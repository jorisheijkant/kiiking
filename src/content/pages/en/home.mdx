## About Kiiking

Kiiking is an extreme sport where athletes stand on a swing and try to loop 360 degrees around the top bar. It originated in Estonia and has grown into an international competitive sport.

<Image src={image1} alt="Kiiking" />

## How It Works

The athlete pumps the swing using their legs and body to build momentum. The goal is to complete a full rotation — the longer the swing arms, the harder the challenge.
